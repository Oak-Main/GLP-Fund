The Medicare GLP-1 Bridge

Medicare’s GLP-1 Bridge is a temporary program that covers certain GLP-1 drugs for eligible people who have Medicare drug coverage. This page sets out what medicare.gov states, and the date we read it.

What Medicare states

Medicare states that the program covers Foundayo tablets, Wegovy injection and tablets, and the Zepbound KwikPen. It does not cover single-dose Zepbound vials or pens. To qualify you must be 18 or older and meet a body mass index requirement, which is lower if you have certain health conditions. The cost is a fixed monthly copayment, shown below with the date we read it.

How we apply it

Our eligibility check follows the criteria and exclusions on medicare.gov, including the exclusion for people whose Medicare drug plan already covers their GLP-1 drug. If you are close to a threshold or unsure which form of the drug you were prescribed, confirm with Medicare at 1-800-MEDICARE before you count on this pathway.

If you are on Medicare, the savings card is not an option

Manufacturer copay and savings cards exclude anyone enrolled in a federal healthcare program. Our tool removes that pathway entirely for Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries rather than showing a low copay you cannot claim. What remains available is this program, standard Part D coverage where your plan covers the indication, and cash-pay direct programs.

Questions people ask about The Medicare GLP-1 Bridge

Who is eligible for the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge?

Medicare states that the program gives eligible beneficiaries a fixed monthly copayment for a covered GLP-1, with a BMI threshold and a further route in at a lower BMI. You are not eligible if you already get GLP-1 drugs covered through your Part D plan, or if you have type 2 diabetes, moderate-to-severe sleep apnea, or fatty liver disease. Confirm your own eligibility with Medicare directly.

Is the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge price confirmed?

Yes. The cost is a fixed monthly copayment, shown below with the date we read it.

Can I use a manufacturer savings card on Medicare?

No. Manufacturer copay and savings cards exclude anyone enrolled in a federal healthcare program. What remains available is this program, standard Part D coverage where your plan covers the indication, and cash-pay direct programs.
