2026-07-30 — Site launch, with no verified prices

The tool, the engine and the full source manifest go live. No price renders as a number, because on this date no figure could be confirmed against a primary source. This entry exists so the gap is on the record from day one rather than discovered later.

  • Published the ranking engine covering 6 medications and 8 payment pathways, with eligibility rules held as data rather than prose.
  • Filed all 45 tracked price figures as unverified or conflicting with a null value. Outbound connections to every primary source (fda.gov, cms.gov, medicare.gov, hhs.gov, trumprx.gov, sec.gov, and all Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk domains) were refused by the network this build runs on, so none could be read directly.
  • Recorded, alongside each unverified figure, what research located and the provenance actually held for it, so a single verification pass can promote it without a code change.
  • Marked the TrumpRx pathway as manually curated: programmatic access to trumprx.gov is refused, and the November 2025 announced figures conflict with later readings of the live platform.
  • Excluded compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide entirely, the current lawful status being unestablished against FDA sources.
  • Suppressed manufacturer savings cards for Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE and VA beneficiaries rather than ranking them lower, so a pathway that cannot be used is not shown as an option.
  • Applied the reported Medicare Bridge sleep-apnea exclusion despite being unable to confirm it, on the grounds that omitting a real exclusion would quote a price to a beneficiary who cannot obtain it.
